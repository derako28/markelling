### Вариант 1
Kim Basinger never signed the contract for Boxing Helena. A jury still priced that at $8,921,694.

She said yes on January 24, 1991. A deal memo followed on February 27. On June 10, the film’s producer, Main Line Pictures, learned she was out.

Here’s the lesson. A missing signature is not a missing obligation. The assumption nobody checked was what her yes was already worth to everyone else.
